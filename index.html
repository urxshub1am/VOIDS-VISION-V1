Either hand grabs first = ANCHOR. For 3D, the other hand may pinch anywhere away from the Anchor to join = MANIPULATOR. AUTO waits for deliberate motion, then locks that secondary pinch to SCALE or ROTATE; FREE preserves simultaneous transforms. Release the Manipulator to choose again while the Anchor keeps holding. Pinch-drag panels to scroll. Both pinches on empty board control workspace zoom/pan.
