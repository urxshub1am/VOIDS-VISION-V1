# VOIDS VISION V1.7 DEV — Experience & Reliability · Phase 1

Development marker for the `v1.7-dev` branch. Phase 1 is limited to Onboarding V2 and real readiness UX: user-triggered camera permission, browser/camera/tracker setup feedback, a readiness-gated Guided Demo, and migration of completed legacy onboarding state.

The released V1.6 interaction engine remains the baseline. Pointer, Spatial one/two-hand transforms, persistence/import safeguards, and low-FPS tracking behavior are intentionally unchanged until this onboarding pass is runtime-tested.
